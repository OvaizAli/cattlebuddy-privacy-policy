Privacy policy

Effective date: 2026-10-16

Account and farm records

CattleBuddy uses your phone number for account access. You may add a recovery email and farm-member details. Farm records can include animal tags, care and milk records, customer and seller names, feed orders and usage, payments, income and expenses. Only enter information you are entitled to use and share with your farm members.

How information is used

Information is used to operate your account, keep farm records, synchronize devices, apply farm permissions, send requested verification codes and diagnose failures. Firebase Authentication and Firestore handle accounts and cloud records. Firebase Storage may handle authorized reports where configured. Firebase App Check checks app integrity. Firebase Crashlytics processes release crash diagnostics. The OTP service runs on Render and uses TextBee to deliver SMS codes. Authorized farm members can access records according to their roles.

Offline storage and notifications

Working copies are stored in SQLite on your phone. Saved records are queued for cloud synchronization when a connection is available. Notification preferences and in-app reminder history are stored locally. Optional device notifications are controlled by your device permissions and app settings. Uninstalling the app removes its local working copy but does not delete your cloud account or farm records.

Security and technical information

The app uses authenticated access, server-side rules and encrypted network connections. Android backup and device transfer of local farm data are disabled. The local SQLite database does not have additional application-level encryption, so protect your phone and screen lock. App integrity checks, OTP delivery and crash diagnostics may process app or device identifiers, error details and security-related request metadata. This release does not contain an advertising SDK.

Retention and deletion

Farm records are retained while they are needed to provide your farm account and its history. Technical records and logs follow the service providers' configured retention controls. You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data through More > Request account deletion, or by contacting the support email below. An in-app request is submitted for processing; it does not instantly erase your account. Support verifies ownership and explains how shared farm records and any legally required retention apply before completing the request. Deactivating an account is not a substitute for fulfilling a deletion request.

Your choices and support

You can correct farm records where the app allows it, manage farm membership according to your role, control device notifications and request account or data deletion. Contact support.cattlebuddy@gmail.com with privacy questions or deletion requests. Include the registered phone number or recovery email needed to identify the account. Never send your password or SMS verification code to support.

Policy changes

This policy applies to CattleBuddy. Changes to how information is handled will be reflected in an updated policy and effective date. The app's public Google Play listing provides the developer's identity and the public policy address.

Optional Zakat records

If you use the optional Zakat feature, its settings, calculation preferences and daily or monthly given statuses synchronize with your farm records. These records can reveal religious practice. The feature is optional and is not used for advertising or religious profiling.

Website hosting

These public information pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information when you visit them, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. These pages do not include advertising, analytics scripts, tracking cookies or a sign-in form.
